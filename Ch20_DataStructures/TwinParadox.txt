The twin paradox is a thought experiment in special relativity that explores the concept of time dilation. It involves two identical twins, one of whom stays on Earth while the other travels into space at a high speed and then returns. According to special relativity, time passes differently for objects in motion relative to each other, so the traveling twin experiences less time than the twin who stays on Earth. As a result, when the traveling twin returns, they find that the twin who remained on Earth has aged more. This apparent paradox highlights the counterintuitive nature of time dilation and demonstrates one of the key principles of special relativity.
